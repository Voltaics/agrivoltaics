Updated Frost Alert Rules 

 

"The app should communicate three separate conclusions:  

1. Likelihood of frost formation: Frost Watch warning/alert  

2. Expected frost type: radiation, advective, or mixed  

3. Likelihood of vine injury as low/medium/high based on growth stage and exposure duration" 

 

Implementing features new app systems is beyond my capabilities, but I can create alerts using the existing tools to follow Mohsen's guidelines: 

 

"I recommend six app levels of Normal, Watch, Warning, Imminent, Event in Progress, and All Clear." 

 

Level 1: Normal  

Display green when all are true:  

• Air temperature above 40°F  

• No unusually rapid cooling  

• Forecast minimum, when available, above 36°F  

• No frost-sensitive growth stage concern 

No notification is needed.  

 

 

Level 2: Frost Watch 

Trigger a Frost Watch when the vines are at bud swell or later and the rule set below is satisfied: 

 

Rule 2A:  

Rapid dry-air cooling (provided numbers are default values adjustable by farmer in app)  

• Air temperature ≤ 42°F 5  

• Cooling rate ≤ −2°F/hour  

• Wind speed < 5 mph  

• Dew-point spread > 5°F  

 

Rule 2B:  

Forecast threshold approach  

• The local forecast or projected temperature is expected to reach the configured Warning or critical threshold within six hours. 

 

This second pattern deserves attention because dry air can permit considerable additional cooling before saturation occurs.  

 

Example notification: 

Frost Watch: Vineyard temperature is falling. Current temperature 39°F, declining 1.6°F/hour. Continue monitoring.  

 

 

Level 3: Frost Warning 

Trigger a Frost Warning when frost-sensitive tissue is present and any of the following combinations persist for at least two consecutive 15-minute readings.  

 

Rule 3A: 

Radiation-frost pattern (provided numbers are default values and adjustable by farmer in the app)  

• Air temperature ≤ 36°F  

• Wind speed ≤ 3 mph  

• Cooling rate ≤ −1°F/hour  

• Night-time or very low light  

• Either:  

o Dew-point spread ≤ 4°F, or  

o Leaf wetness has changed from dry to wet  

This is the classic clear/calm-night pattern. Wind machines are most useful under calm conditions when a temperature inversion exists.  

 

Rule 3B: 

Rapid dry-air cooling (provided numbers are default values and adjustable by farmer in the app) 

• Air temperature ≤ 37°F  

• Wind speed ≤ 5 mph  

• Cooling rate ≤ −2°F/hour  

• Dew-point-spread > 4°F (see Appendix A) 6  

• Projected temperature reaches 32°F within two hours  

The projected temperature can initially be calculated as: 𝑇𝑝𝑟𝑜𝑗𝑒𝑐𝑡𝑒𝑑,2ℎ = 𝑇𝑛𝑜𝑤 + 2(𝐶𝑜𝑜𝑙𝑖𝑛𝑔𝑅𝑎𝑡𝑒60)  

That is deliberately simple. Later, we can replace it with a locally calibrated regression using the vineyard’s recorded nights.  

 

Rule 3C: 

Wind-driven freeze approaching signaling possible Advective freeze (provided numbers are default values and adjustable by farmer in the app)  

• Air temperature ≤ 36°F  

• Wind speed consistently > 5 mph  

• Temperature continuing to fall  

• Regional weather forecast indicates freezing air  

This should be labeled Advective Freeze Warning, not radiation frost. High winds signal an incoming cold-air mass and can make irrigation protection or high-speed fans less effective. So, in this situation we will have two distinct warning, on for radiation freeze and one for advective freeze.  

 

Example notifications are:  

Radiation Freeze Warning: Bud-zone temperature is 35°F and falling. Wind is calm, and conditions favor surface frost. The configured critical temperature of 32°F may be reached within two hours. Prepare the approved frost-protection procedure.  

Advective Freeze Warning: A regional freeze is approaching. Bud-zone temperature is 35°F and falling, sustained wind is 8.2 mph, and the forecast indicates temperatures below the configured critical threshold within two hours. Wind-based mixing may provide limited protection. 

 

 

Level 4: Frost Imminent 

Trigger Frost Imminent if any of the following occurs.  

 

Rule 4A:  

Critical air-temperature approach (provided numbers are default values and adjustable by farmer in the app)  

• Air temperature ≤ 33°F  

• Cooling rate remains negative  

• Bud break or later growth stage  

 

Rule 4B:  

Frost forming on wet tissue  

(provided numbers are default values and adjustable by farmer in the app)  

• Air temperature ≤ 33°F 7  

• Leaf wetness is active  

• Dew-point spread ≤ 1°F  

• Wind speed ≤ 3 mph  

Trigger if: 𝑇𝑏𝑢𝑑_𝑧𝑜𝑛𝑒 ≤ 32°𝐹  

 

Rule 4C:  

Bud-zone temperature reaches the configured critical threshold  

Trigger Frost Imminent when the latest valid bud-zone temperature reaches or falls below the farmer-configured critical temperature (provided numbers are default values and adjustable by farmer in the app)  

• 𝑇𝑏𝑢𝑑_𝑧𝑜𝑛𝑒 ≤ 32°F for the last reading  

 

Example notification:  

Frost Imminent: Measured bud-zone temperature has reached 31°F. Initiate the vineyard’s frost protection procedure immediately. 

 

 

Level 5: Freeze Event in Progress 

Trigger when either:  

• The measured bud-zone temperature remains at or below the default value of 32°F or farmer-configured critical temperature for the farmer-configured minimum exposure duration or the default of 30 minutes; or  

• The measured bud-zone temperature falls at least 2°F below the configured critical threshold in one valid reading. The 2°F margin is an adjustable default.  

Display:  

• Time below the configured threshold by farmer  

• Time below 32°F  

• Time below 30°F  

• Continuous leaf wetness  

• Wind classification  

• Growth stage  

• Vineyard zones affected  

 

Freeze duration matters because longer exposure increases tissue injury.  

 

Cornell notes that grape shoots may survive a brief freeze but suffer more severe damage when freezing persists and ice formation disrupts tissue.  

 

Example notification:  

8 Freeze Event in Progress: Bud-zone temperature has remained below the configured critical threshold for 30 minutes. Continue the vineyard’s approved frost-protection procedure and monitoring. 

 

 

Level 6: All Clear 

Monitoring should continue past sunrise and then the app should report the likelihood of vine injury based on the exposure duration. Also, do not lower an alert level until:  

• Temperature is at least 2°F above the applicable critical threshold;  

• Temperature has been stable or rising for at least 30 minutes; and  

• No forecasted decline below the critical threshold is expected during the next hour.  

 

Issue All Clear only after those conditions are met.  

Also, continue logging after All Clear so the app can calculate:  

• Minimum temperature  

• Time below the critical threshold  

• Time below 32°F  

• Maximum cooling rate  

• Wetness duration  

• Zones affected  

 

Example notification:  

All Clear: The current frost event has ended. Bud-zone temperature is above the configured critical threshold and has remained stable or rising for 30 minutes. Continue routine monitoring.
